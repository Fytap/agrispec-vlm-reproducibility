\section{S1. Analysis chronology and truth-access governance}

WeedsGalore and CWFID serve different evidential roles. WeedsGalore supports internal development on its official train, validation, and test split. Several extended WeedsGalore analyses were conducted after the initial official-test archive existed and are interpreted as within-dataset engineering analyses. CWFID was separately frozen and opened once, without external fitting, threshold selection, or policy search. Table~S1 records this chronology. Test truth was unavailable to every queue-forming stage and entered only the offline scorer.

\section{S4. Proposal definitions, operating-point selection, and matching sensitivity}

For truth instance $I$, candidate $C$, all labelled plant pixels $L$, and pixels of agricultural role $R$, the offline scorer records
\[
q_I=\frac{|I\cap C|}{|I|},\qquad
q_L=\frac{|C\cap L|}{|C|},\qquad
q_R=\frac{|C\cap R|}{|C\cap L|}.
\]
Spatial support requires $q_I\ge0.50$. Role-qualified support additionally requires $q_L\ge0.50$, $q_R\ge0.90$, and a role match. One-to-one recovery uses a maximum-cardinality bipartite matching over eligible candidate--instance edges. Candidate identifiers and then truth identifiers define the deterministic lexicographic tie order.
