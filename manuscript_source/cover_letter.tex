\begin{letter}{Editors-in-Chief\\Smart Agricultural Technology}
\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the manuscript, ``Bounded Instance-Support Evaluation for Finite Crop--Weed Review Queues,'' for publication in \emph{Smart Agricultural Technology}.

The study addresses an applied quality-assurance question for agricultural vision: when only a finite number of predicted regions can be reviewed, how much distinct-plant support survives candidate formation, role qualification, ranking, and budget truncation? We organise these stages into an executable evaluation contract for finite crop--weed review queues.

The contract separates spatial support, role-qualified support, merge-aware set coverage, and one-to-one support. The two final endpoints bound geometric plant support when one region creates one quality-assurance record. On the official WeedsGalore split, proposal commissioning nearly doubled spatial support (0.2973 to 0.5713) and raised role-qualified support by 0.1098 to 0.3026. A 27-contract analysis further showed that the queued effect depends on the proposal distribution used to train the ranker.

The model study compares U-Net, DeepLabV3+, and Mask R-CNN under the same $K=20$ capacity. It includes crossed training-seed and test-image uncertainty, standard AP and AR, a queue-level PQ-style diagnostic, contract-rank stability, and crop-exposure sensitivity. Mask R-CNN achieved the highest one-to-one point estimate (0.2159); the crossed seed-by-image contrast with U-Net was 0.0132 (95\% interval, $-0.0009$ to 0.0300).

Two independent operators reviewed the same 518-region queue and reached 0.9421 agreement on weed reviewability (Cohen's $\kappa=0.8930$). Merge-aware analysis identified 453 set-covered instances but 348 one-to-one matches, exposing a 105-instance capacity gap that AP, AR, and queue-level PQ-style summaries did not express.

A cross-dataset transfer applied five validation-selected U-Net checkpoints to 60 CWFID images without external fitting. Validation-selected thresholds exceeded the external foreground responses; continuous-score diagnostics located the shift in ranking and threshold response. This experiment connects the finite-queue endpoint to an explicit external-calibration requirement.

The accompanying GitHub release contains figure source data, configurations, per-image and per-seed outputs, matching tables, model and data hashes, preserved run records, and replay programs. The compact verifier reproduces the archived tables and numerical invariants; public-data and model-acquisition instructions support full retraining.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all authors.
