Figure 1. Information architecture of the support-aware queue analysis. (a) The RGB-only path separates candidate formation, role qualification, ranking, allocation, and group-stratified queue auditing. (b) Source supervision and the official WeedsGalore 104/26/26 spatial roles; the 26-tile test set comprises two held-out spatial patches observed across four acquisition dates. (c) Truth-access contract. Training truth supports proposal search and model selection, validation truth is restricted to setting selection, queue formation uses only RGB and model outputs, and test truth is accessed after a queue is locked for offline scoring.

(b) End-to-end comparison at K=20 using identical all-candidate role-plus-local weights. (c) Candidate-pool composition. Crop exposure is the frequency of images containing any queued candidate with nonzero crop overlap.
